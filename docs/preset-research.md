# Preset research seeds

Updated on 2026-10-24. The bounded-software source record now supports one structurally validated preset; other entries remain research seeds. Source practice, the proposed Hakengaisya adaptation and executable validation are separate evidence levels.

## Software work at different scales

[Basecamp: Adjust to Your Size](https://basecamp.com/shapeup/4.1-appendix-02) distinguishes common principles from scale-dependent practices. Its small-team example combines responsibilities and alternates shaping/building, while its larger-team discussion introduces more specialized support and coordination. It explicitly treats the time window and practices as adaptable.

Proposed adoption: a micro preset can combine compatible continuous work, while larger scale overlays separate actual streams and support capabilities. Define scope and stop conditions without imposing a fixed six-week cadence or reproducing Basecamp's hierarchy. Hakengaisya retains direct-supervisor acceptance even when source practices describe informal collaboration.

Delivered in 0.8.8: the [bounded-software preset](../presets/bounded-software.json) and [its usage/verification record](bounded-software-preset.md). Its source review also covers finishable scopes, explicit stop conditions and evidence-based code review. Service delivery and the other software families still need their own source-specific workflows, scale interfaces and negative paths.

## Product discovery and delivery

[GitLab: Product Processes](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/product/product-processes/#product-development-flow) describes a dual-track development flow across product, engineering, UX and quality, with some overlapping steps and ongoing product work. This is a company operating reference, not a prescription to start one agent per discipline or step.

Proposed adoption: make discovery findings and delivery dependencies explicit, permit genuinely independent work to overlap and keep one accountable owner for each result. A hypothesis or discussion artifact cannot satisfy a gate that requires observed user validation. Internal department names and GitLab's issue tooling are not copied into the runtime.

Still missing: the relevant detailed flow and decision-right contracts need further review, followed by project/scale variants and executable expansion to the existing controller. The separate collaboration-model page returned a retrieval error during this review and was not used as evidence.

## Data and database ownership

[Thoughtworks: Break Through the Centralized Platform Bottlenecks with Data Mesh](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/articles/break-though-the-centralized-platform-bottlenecks-with-data-mesh) describes data as domain-owned products with shared interoperability governance and discusses problems created by a centralized platform bottleneck.

Proposed adoption: identify the actual data owner, consumer contract, quality evidence and cross-stream integration responsibilities. Keep small database work compact; a migration or query correction does not automatically need data-mesh teams or a platform department. No throughput or cost claim from the source is transferred to Hakengaisya.

Still missing: separate analysis/pipeline/migration workflows, access boundaries, rollback, data-quality acceptance and scale-specific validation. This seed is not a completed data preset or proof that a model may access real data.
