# Hakengaisya development rules

- Read ROADMAP.md before changing this project and update its current work, gaps and completed evidence when the scope changes.
- Keep Hakengaisya on 0.8.x while completing the required researched workflow presets and their applicable scale variants. Use patch releases; do not advance to 0.9 because organization diagrams or module examples pass tests.
- Enter 0.9 only after the 0.8 exit checklist is satisfied. Use 0.9 for real-task validation and optimization. Enter 1.0 only after the real-task acceptance and operational readiness gates pass.
- Every preset needs documented company/practitioner sources, applicability, actual deliverables, reporting and direct-supervisor acceptance, correction paths, scale rules and evidence requirements. Follow docs/preset-contract.md.
- Derive organization size from work and review load. Preserve explicit superior-subordinate authority, independent checking and producer-versus-acceptor separation when combining responsibilities.
- Keep proposal validation, simulated control flow, real model/tool execution and user acceptance distinct. Do not mark an unchecked roadmap item complete based on a weaker evidence level.
- The evaluator remains independently usable. Its component version can differ from the suite milestone; keep its privacy and evidence boundaries intact.
- Keep new files and technical artifacts in English. Preserve private local histories and credentials outside the publication set. Follow the current user's authorization and host delegation limits.
